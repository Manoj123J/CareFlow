Basic First-Aid Knowledge

General instructions while professional emergency services are en route.

Burns First-Aid

  1. Immediately cool the burn under running cool tap water for 10 to 20 minutes.
  2. Do not apply ice, butter, toothpaste, or greasy ointments to the area.
  3. Remove tight jewelry or restrictive clothing before swelling develops.
  4. Cover loosely with a clean, dry, non-stick dressing or clean plastic wrap.

Adult CPR

  1. Check responsiveness and breathing. Call 108 immediately.
  2. Place heel of one hand in center of chest; interlock other hand on top.
  3. Push hard and fast: 100 to 120 compressions per minute at 2 inches depth.
  4. Allow chest to recoil completely between compressions until help arrives.

Choking Response

  1. Encourage the person to cough if they are still able to make sounds.
  2. If airway is blocked, stand behind them and lean them slightly forward.
  3. Deliver 5 firm back blows between shoulder blades with heel of hand.
  4. If unrelieved, perform 5 upward abdominal thrusts (Heimlich maneuver).

Suspected Fracture

  1. Keep the injured limb still. Do not attempt to straighten the bone.
  2. Support and immobilize the area using a soft splint or folded towel.
  3. Apply an ice pack wrapped in cloth to reduce swelling (avoid direct ice).
  4. Await professional transport and medical evaluation.

Bleeding Control

  1. Apply firm, steady direct pressure over wound using a clean cloth or pad.
  2. Keep constant pressure on the site without lifting the cloth to check.
  3. If blood soaks through, place another cloth on top without removing first.
  4. Have person rest comfortably lying down while waiting for an ambulance.
